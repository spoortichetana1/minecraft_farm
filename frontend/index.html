SurvivorCraft 3D

You are a lone survivor in a dangerous world. Gather supplies during the day. Survive monsters at night.

How To Play

Controls

  • WASD / Arrows Move
  • Camera Follows movement
  • Click Chop / Attack With Axe
  • Double Click Hunt With Axe
  • Eat Restore Hunger
  • Right Click Build Stone Wall
  • 1-6 Select Tool / Block

Goals

  • Gather resources
  • Collect meat
  • Eat to manage hunger
  • Build shelter
  • Survive monsters at night
