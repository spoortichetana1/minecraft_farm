SurvivorCraft 3D

You are a lone survivor in a dangerous world. Gather supplies during the day. Survive monsters at night.

How To Play

Controls

  • WASD / Arrows Move
  • Camera Follows movement
  • Click Use Tool / Chop
  • Double Click Hunt / Collect Meat
  • Right Click Place Block
  • 1-6 Select Tool / Block

Goals

  • Gather resources
  • Collect meat
  • Build shelter
  • Survive monsters at night
