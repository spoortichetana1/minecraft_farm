FarmCraft 3D

You are a lone farmer surviving in a dangerous world. Farm during the day. Survive monsters at night.

How To Play

Controls

  • WASD Move
  • Mouse Look Around
  • Left Click Break Block
  • Right Click Place Block
  • 1-5 Select Block

Goals

  • Gather resources
  • Farm crops
  • Build shelter
  • Survive monsters at night
