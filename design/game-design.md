# SurvivorCraft Design Notes

SurvivorCraft is a browser-based 3D survival game where the player gathers resources, hunts animals, builds shelter, and survives dangerous nights.

## Core Loop

1. Explore the world.
2. Chop trees for wood with the axe.
3. Hunt animals for meat.
4. Build shelter and light with collected resources.
5. Survive night threats.
6. Repeat with better positioning, shelter, and inventory.

## Controls

- Move with `W`, `A`, `S`, `D` or arrow keys.
- Single left click chops nearby trees.
- Double left click hunts nearby animals and collects meat.
- Right click builds a protective stone wall.
- Wood, meat, and stone can be traded for health.
- Number keys select hotbar items.

## Out Of Scope

Farming, seeds, wheat, crop growth, crop harvesting, and farm progression are intentionally out of scope. The player fantasy is survivor, not farmer.
